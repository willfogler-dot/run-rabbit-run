The two numbers that matter

Olympian Hall, mile 51.2, 2:30 AM. The tightest cumulative gate on the course. 17h30 for 51.2 miles is a 20:31/mi average — faster than the 21:14/mi needed to finish in 36:00. It is the only cutoff where a runner capable of finishing can still get pulled. Aim for midnight to 1:00 AM.

Long Lake, mile 89.0, by about 5:00 PM Saturday. The posted 6:30 PM cutoff is not survivable. From there on the line you would need 6.8 uphill miles in 90 minutes and then 6 downhill miles in 60 minutes, at miles 92 and 99 of a 36-hour race. The late cutoffs are formalities that let the sweeps pull you.

Section by section

Three ways to get Will disqualified
  1. Do not drive to Fish Creek Falls Trailhead. Foot or bike only, 4 mi from Olympian Hall.
  2. Do not crew or drive above Dry Lake on Buffalo Pass Road. Summit Lake is the 2-hour route around, every time.
  3. Do not park at Dry Lake. Drop off, turn around, leave. The sheriff tickets and tows.

Driving

Why Summit at mile 30 is skipped

The drop bag covers it, and a four-hour round trip at mile 30 wrecks the crew before the stops that matter. Note the split: Dry Lake has crew but no drop bag, so those two visits are the crew's highest-value work. Summit has a drop bag, so it can absorb a miss.

Rules

Pacers may join or leave only at Dry Lake 44.5, Olympian 51.2, Olympian 63.9, Dry Lake 70.8, and Summit 80.8. One pacer at a time. Unlimited family for the last ~4 miles from the top of the gondola — they may have to hike up. Pacers get themselves to and from aid stations. No headphones between Spring Creek Trailhead and Olympian Hall in either direction.

Confirm at Thursday's briefing. The manual contradicts itself on the first pickup: §5 and the aid station table say Dry Lake 44.5; §14 prose says Spring Creek Trailhead. Two sources to one, so this plan uses 44.5.
Simpler alternative — one vehicle

Pacer 1 runs 44.5 → 70.8 (26.3 mi). Pacer 2 runs 70.8 → finish (31.0 mi). This eliminates both two-hour Summit drives entirely. It costs Pacer 2 a very long night.

Mandatory gear — new for 2026

0/5

Checked on the way out of Olympian Hall. Olympian is mile 51.2 and 63.9 — you carry all five for the final 50.6 miles.

Drop bags

Collected Thursday 3–6 PM, before the briefing. Both bags are sealed roughly 15 hours before the start. You cannot adjust them to Friday's forecast. Pack for the bad version.

No hard-sided bags. Nothing bigger than a shoebox. Label with bib, name, station. Bring a bright colour — you will be finding it in a sea of identical bags.
